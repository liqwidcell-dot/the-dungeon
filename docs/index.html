Built for epic runs

Everything you need for an intense dungeon experience

👥

Co-op Parties

Team up with up to 4 players. Form parties with invites, share the arena, and fight together through all 10 waves.

📈

Escalating Waves

10 waves of increasingly difficult mobs, culminating in an epic battle against the custom Dungeon Lord boss.

💀

Individual Elimination

Die and lose only your dungeon loot - your personal inventory stays safe. The run continues for surviving teammates.

🎁

Smart Loot System

Dungeon items are tagged and only those are lost on death. Earn rewards each wave with tiered loot tables that improve over time.

⚖️

Balanced Scaling

Mob counts scale with party size while health and damage scale by wave. A carefully tuned difficulty curve keeps it challenging.

🏰

Protected Arena

Fixed sky arena with full protection - no breaking, placing, or exploiting the walls. Pure skill-based gameplay.

Simple Commands

Easy to learn, powerful to use

/dungeon Start a run solo or with your party
/dungeon invite <player> Invite someone to your party
/dungeon join Accept a pending party invite
/dungeon party View your party or run status
/dungeon leave Bail out and keep your gear
/dungeon info Check current run progress
/dungeon demo Open the demo hub GUI
