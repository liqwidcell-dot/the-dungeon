Features

🌊 Wave System

10 escalating waves of mobs with increasing difficulty

👥 Co-op Parties

Team up with up to 4 players for epic battles

👹 Boss Fight

Face off against the custom Dungeon Lord

💰 Smart Loot

Dungeon items are tagged and only lost on death

💀 Individual Elimination

Your gear is safe - only dungeon loot is at risk

⚖️ Balanced Scaling

Difficulty scales with party size and wave count
